Joining …

PixelPlus is switching to your network. This phone will be disconnected from the setup hotspot in a few seconds.

  1. Reconnect this phone to your Wi-Fi.
  2. Wait about a minute, then open:
http://pixelplus.local

If that address does not open (some Android phones and Windows PCs don't support .local names), look for pixelplus in your router's list of devices and open its IP address instead.

Wrong password? The PixelPlus hotspot comes back within a minute — just join it again.

Using PixelPlus without Wi-Fi

http://10.42.0.1

You can connect to Wi-Fi later in Settings → Network.
